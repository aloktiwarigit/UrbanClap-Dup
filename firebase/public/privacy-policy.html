Privacy Policy for Home Heroo

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Home Heroo is operated by Alok Tiwari. This policy applies to the Home Heroo Customer app (in.homeheroo.customer) and Home Heroo Technician app (in.homeheroo.technician).

Information We Collect

We collect information needed to provide home service booking, technician assignment, job completion, payments, support, safety, and account functionality.

This may include:

  • Name, phone number, email address, and account identifiers
  • Service address and location information
  • Booking details, job status, ratings, complaints, and support messages
  • Photos captured for technician KYC, job evidence, service quality, or complaint resolution
  • Camera access in the technician app to capture KYC and job-related photos
  • Payment and payout references, such as Razorpay payment IDs or payout records
  • Device identifiers such as Firebase user ID and push notification tokens
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data for reliability and security

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Authenticate users
  • Create and manage bookings
  • Assign technicians
  • Track active service jobs
  • Process payments and payouts
  • Verify technician identity and service evidence
  • Send booking, job, payment, and safety notifications
  • Handle ratings, complaints, refunds, and support
  • Improve reliability, security, and fraud prevention
  • Meet legal, tax, audit, and regulatory obligations

Camera and Photo Usage

The technician app uses camera access only for app features such as KYC, job stage evidence, completion photos, and complaint or service documentation. We do not use camera data for advertising.

Location Usage

Location may be used for service address selection, dispatch, live job tracking, technician routing, safety monitoring, and service fulfilment. Technician background location may be used only during active job flows where it is necessary for dispatch, tracking, and safety.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share necessary data with service providers used to operate the platform, including Firebase, Google Cloud, Azure, Razorpay, Sentry, DigiLocker, and similar infrastructure or compliance providers. We may also share data when legally required.

Data Security

Data is transmitted using encryption in transit. Access is limited to operational and compliance needs.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only as long as needed for service operation, legal compliance, payment records, tax records, dispute handling, safety, and audit obligations. Users may request access to or deletion of their personal data by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Alok Tiwari at aloktiwari49@gmail.com.
